1. OVERVIEW
WHAT THE PORTAL IS
The Family Portal is your private, secure home base for everything Safe Steps built for your family. Log in with your Family ID and you have on-demand access to your actual plan documents, your equipment, your drill history, and a live practice tool built around your real floor plan.
Think of it as the difference between a plan sitting in a drawer and a plan your family actually uses. It's built to stay current as your family does: new equipment, new drills, a new layout, a new season of life.
2. CAPABILITIES
WHAT IT DOES
01
YOUR DOCUMENTS, ON DEMAND

Plan & Document Library

Every document we build for your family, your fire safety plan, first responder sheet, fridge card, and any other deliverable, lives in one place, tied to your Family ID. No digging through email for a PDF you saved two years ago.

02
PRACTICE ON YOUR ACTUAL HOME

Live Scenario Generator

The portal reads your family's real floor plan and drops a randomized fire origin point and family member positions onto it. Instead of rehearsing a generic drill, your family walks through an escape decision based on your actual layout, a new scenario every time.

03
TRACK YOUR READINESS

Drill History & Scoring

Log every rehearsal your family runs, see how you scored, and track improvement over time. It's the difference between assuming your family is prepared and actually knowing it.

04
STAY AHEAD OF FAILURE POINTS

Equipment & Maintenance Tracker

Log the smoke alarms, extinguishers, and safety equipment your family owns: where it's located, when it was purchased, when it was last serviced. The portal tracks manufacturer maintenance schedules and lifespans so a dead battery or an expired extinguisher never catches you off guard.

3. PURPOSE
WHY IT EXISTS
Rehearsal builds muscle memory
Knowing a plan and running a plan are two different things. Repeated rehearsal, with scoring, history, and a live scenario tool, is what turns a plan on paper into instinct your family can rely on under real stress.
Your equipment is your first line of defense
A smoke alarm with a dead battery or an expired extinguisher fails you exactly when you need it most. Tracking what you own and when it was last serviced is how that first line of defense stays ready.
